02 / COMMUNITYMEET GROUND'D

Come meet the community

Ground'd starts with people in a room. We bring together people making or supporting art in North Texas: painters, designers, musicians, creative technologists, students, organizers and the curious.

Bring what you're working on, what you need, or just yourself. Connect with a community of people working across disciplines.

03 / THE WORKWHAT WE'RE BUILDING

What we're building

Ground'd connects Texas artists and cultural organizations with each other and with the resources they need to keep making work here.

  • Open meetups

    Regular, free gatherings where creative people across disciplines find collaborators, jobs and friends.

  • A Texas quarterly

    A print and digital publication featuring Texas art, artists, culture and the issues shaping them.

  • Student showcase

    A juried showcase with scholarships for high school, college and continuing-ed artists.
